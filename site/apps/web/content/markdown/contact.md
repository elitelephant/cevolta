# Contact

Cevolta doesn't have a support inbox yet. It's a solo, pre-launch
project, so there are two real ways to reach it, and both go straight
to the person building it, not a team or a ticket queue.

## GitHub

Open an issue on the [repository](https://github.com/elitelephant/cevolta)
for bug reports, protocol questions, or anything you think should be
documented. This is the fastest way to get a direct answer, and it's
public, so the answer helps the next person with the same question.

## Waitlist

[Join the waitlist](https://cevolta.xyz/#waitlist) and you'll be the
first to hear when Cevolta opens on Testnet. It's also a way to signal
that you want to hear more, even without a specific question.

There's no chatbot and no support ticket system behind this page — just
those two channels.
