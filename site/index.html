Every recurring-payment pattern today puts the risk on the subscriber

Cevolta exists because both common patterns get this backwards.

Common pattern

Open-ended allowance

You approve a merchant's contract to pull funds until you remember to revoke it. If that contract is ever compromised, an attacker can drain the whole allowance in one transaction.

Common pattern

Manual approval every cycle

No open allowance, but no automation either — you're back to approving a transaction every billing period, the exact friction recurring payments are supposed to remove.

The permission lives in your wallet, not the merchant's contract

A Policy Signer inside your Smart Wallet — not a shared streaming or pooling contract — authorizes only what you set: this merchant, this ceiling, this cadence. Even a compromised merchant contract can't spend outside your rule.

You set the limit once

Amount, recipient, and cadence — enforced by your own wallet, not by the merchant's word.

Renewals need no signature

An off-chain trigger asks your wallet to charge on schedule. Your Policy Signer allows or rejects it — no human in the loop.

Cancel is instant and yours

One action, from your own wallet, shuts off future charges atomically. No ticket, no approval, no waiting.

How it works

One protocol, two sides. Here's exactly what each one does.

For merchants

Get paid, without custody
1

Create a plan

Set price, currency, and billing cadence. Get a checkout link — no code required.

2

Get paid automatically

Funds land in your wallet every cycle. No intermediary ever holds custody or can freeze it.

3

See subscribers in real time

Track who's active, past due, or cancelled — reads straight from the Subscription Registry.

For subscribers

One signature, full control
1

Subscribe once

Confirm the plan's limits and install a Policy Signer in your Smart Wallet — the only signature you'll ever give this merchant.

2

Let renewals happen

A Renewal Trigger asks your wallet to charge on schedule. Your Policy Signer checks the rule and decides — automatically.

3

Cancel in one click

Revoke the Policy Signer from your own wallet, anytime. Effective immediately — no merchant involved.
